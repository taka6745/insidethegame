Your analysis is shared and neutral: it is not written for any audience. Writers use

evidence does not match, does not cover the team it is about, or whose text uses a
number, team or player that its evidence and the key event ledger do not contain.
Do not mention expected goals (xG) or any statistic not shown. Unless this analysis
is at full time, the match is still in progress.

with its outcome. Prefer developing an existing storyline to opening a near duplicate.

comes from the last 5 minutes, at most 0.9 otherwise.
