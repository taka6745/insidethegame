## Our current recommendation

**A live story desk that can say “we don't know yet,” and correct itself when the feed changes.** Give a producer a useful explanation, the supporting events, a fan/analyst view, and a clear publish/retract action. Pair it with a few original synthetic scenarios that test whether its stories hold up. See the [candidate comparison](business.md#candidate-directions).

This fits an existing distribution chain: data provider → production team → broadcaster or fan platform. Generic chat, multilingual recaps, player tracking, and personalised highlights already exist. The proposed difference needs to be demonstrated through story quality, correction behaviour, or reduced producer work—not asserted as a new category.

The user’s **working hypothesis** is lightweight idea/research sourcing. Commercial reuse terms and the partnership make that plausible; the public materials do not establish internal intent or a purchasing commitment. [Business evidence](business.md#is-this-idea-sourcing)
